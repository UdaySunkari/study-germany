Why Germany

Great degrees,
without the debt.

Why Germany has quietly become the smartest-value destination for Indian students.

Free public universities

Most public universities charge no tuition, even for non-EU students — just a small semester fee (~€150–350), often including a transport pass.

  • No tuition at most public unis
  • Small semester contribution
  • Baden-Württemberg charges non-EU ~€1,500/sem

English-taught Master's

Hundreds of programmes are fully in English. German isn't needed for admission, though some helps daily life and work.

  • 100s of English programmes
  • No German needed to get in
  • A1–A2 helps for jobs & life

Engineering & tech

CS, Data Science, Mechanical, Electrical, Automotive, Renewable Energy — with deep industry links that mean internships and jobs.

  • TU9 technical universities
  • Universities of Applied Sciences
  • Strong industry pipeline

Work while you study

Students can work part-time to help cover living costs and build local experience for after graduation.

  • Part-time work allowed
  • Helps cover living costs
  • Builds local experience

18-month stay-back

After graduating, an 18-month job-seeker permit to find skilled work — with a clear route to longer-term residence.

  • 18-month job-seeker visa
  • Pathway to PR
  • Real post-study prospects

Two intakes a year

Winter (October) is the big one; Summer (April) is smaller and less competitive. We help you target the right one.

  • Winter: October (main)
  • Summer: April (smaller)
  • We plan your timeline

At most public universities, yes — no tuition fee, even for Indian students. You pay a small semester contribution (often ~€150–350) that often includes a transport pass. The exception is Baden-Württemberg, which charges non-EU students roughly €1,500/semester. Private universities charge full fees, and we'll always tell you which is which.
The APS verifies your Indian academic documents, and it's mandatory for almost every Indian student applying for a German student visa for a degree programme. It currently costs ₹18,000 (non-refundable) and takes about 6–10 weeks, so it's the very first thing to start — before applications. We guide you through it.
For 2026 you must show €11,904 for the year (€992/month) in a blocked account (Sperrkonto). It's your own money — once you arrive in Germany it's released back to you monthly for living costs. Providers like Fintiba and Expatrio are commonly used. Open it before your visa appointment.
For an English-taught Master's, usually not for admission. But some German (A1–A2) makes daily life, part-time jobs and settling in much easier, and helps with jobs after graduation. We'll tell you honestly how much your path needs.
