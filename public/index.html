What we do

Every step,
handled with care.

From the first conversation about your shortlist to the moment you land. We work in writing, on calls, and quietly behind the scenes.

Course discovery

A shortlist that actually fits — your grades, your budget, your future career, and the country you'd thrive in.

  • Career-first matching
  • Ranked university list
  • Honest fit analysis

Application drafting

Personal statements and SOPs written with you, not for you. We push back, ask hard questions, and end up with something that actually sounds like you on your best day.

  • SOP & personal statements
  • LOR coordination
  • Portfolio & CV review

Visa & documentation

The paperwork that scares everyone else. Financials, biometrics, interviews, GTE statements — sequenced into a checklist that won't surprise you at 11pm.

  • Country-specific visa filing
  • Mock interview prep
  • Document vault

Scholarships

We map every funding source you qualify for and write applications that don't read like everyone else's.

  • Merit & need-based
  • Country-of-origin grants
  • Tuition-waiver tracking

Test prep

IELTS, TOEFL, GRE, GMAT, PTE — bookings, study plans, and partner tutors at student rates.

Pre-departure

Forex, SIM cards, accommodation shortlists, airport pickup, and a Slack channel with last year's students.

Career & PR

Post-study work visas, CV translation, alumni intros, and PR roadmaps for the countries that offer one.

APS certificate

Indian students need APS verification before applying to German universities. We handle the documents, the portal, and the timing so it never holds up your application.

How it works

From first call
to first semester.

A five-stage process that takes between four and ten months depending on your destination. We sit with you the whole way.
01

Discovery call

A free 45-minute conversation. We listen first, recommend second.

02

Shortlist & plan

A ranked list of universities and a calendar with every deadline plotted.

03

Application

SOPs, references, transcripts, portfolios — drafted, reviewed, submitted.

04

Offers & visa

Negotiate scholarships, accept the right offer, file a watertight visa.

05

Land & settle

Accommodation, bank account, SIM, orientation — sorted before you land.
